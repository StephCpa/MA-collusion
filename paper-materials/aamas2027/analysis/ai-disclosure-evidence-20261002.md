# AI-use disclosure evidence — 2026-10-02

This note separates the AI system studied in the experiments from AI-assisted
authoring, because the AAMAS policy treats them differently.

## Experimental system (documented)

The manuscript and protocol record the tested deployment as `deepseek-flash`,
with temperature 0.3, a 256-token limit, a forced price-selection tool and one
format attempt without repair. The system prompt and user-message fields are
described in `latex/history-display-observability.tex`, §4. These details are
part of the experimental method, not an AI co-author claim.

## Authoring or methodology assistance (not yet author-certified)

The public candidate does not contain a dated inventory of any AI assistant
used by the authors during hypothesis formation, experimental design, coding or
prose editing. Therefore this repository does **not** certify that the AAMAS
AI-disclosure requirement is satisfied. Before submission, the authors should
record, outside the anonymous manuscript if necessary:

1. tool/provider and exact model or version;
2. whether it contributed to hypotheses or experimental methodology;
3. the relevant prompts or a reproducible prompt summary; and
4. which outputs were independently checked by the authors.

Do not infer these fields from the experimental model metadata. If the authors
decide that the policy does not require a disclosure for their use, record that
decision and its rationale in the submission checklist.
