# Structural primary: exact sign-test robustness check

The registered structural primary has 42 complete blocks. Every block-level
natural-minus-hide-rival contrast is positive (42 positive, 0 zero, 0
negative). Treating the non-zero block signs as exchangeable under a sharp
zero-median null gives the exact two-sided sign-test probability

\[
2(1/2)^{42}=4.547\times 10^{-13}.
\]

This is a low-assumption descriptive robustness check, not a replacement for
the registered block-$t$ interval or the completion-range analysis. It does
not address missing blocks, randomisation validity, or the interpretation of
repeated rounds within a block. The primary conclusion remains the registered
one: the structural contrast is positive and its full completion range stays
above zero.
