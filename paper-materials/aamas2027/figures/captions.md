# Figure captions

## Figure 1. Aggregate-equivalent states and asymmetric-cell structure

**(A)** The 100 ordered joint price states on the `{2.0, 2.5, ..., 6.5}` grid collapse into 10 classes under minimum-price welfare observables. **(B)** The attainable profit-HHI endpoints within each minimum-price class; only 0.50 and 1.00 occur under the two-seller tie-split rule, and the 6.5 class is a singleton. **(C–D)** Assessment-round counts for the LH asymmetric initial cell under natural display and hidden rival history. Rows are seller B prices and columns are seller A prices. Counts are repeated-round summaries, not independent observations.

## Figure 2. Welfare and structure answer different questions (revised 2026-10-01)

**(A)** Registered welfare contrasts `D_asym` and `J` (hide-rival minus natural) for the original controlled-initial run and the Candidate A replication, with Bonferroni block-level intervals; light bars are missing-outcome completion ranges (bounds, not confidence intervals) and the shaded band is the protocol's candidate margin `delta_W = 5`. **(B)** Structural contrasts in the assessment-window equal-price fraction (natural minus hide-rival) with 95% block-t intervals for LH and HL in both runs, and the replication's protocol primary `D_tie`. HHI is omitted because under the tie-split rule its contrast is exactly -1/2 times the equal-price contrast. The earlier version of this figure also plotted the four-arm study's post-hoc simple contrast, which is a different study and estimand; it is now reported only in the text.

## Figure 3. Matching direction decides whether structure foreshadows welfare (new 2026-10-01)

**(A)** Tie-formation events with rival prices visible, drawn as diverging bars: downward events (the high seller cuts to the low price) left of zero, upward events (the low seller raises to the high price) right. Programmed starts are the Candidate A replication's LH and HL natural-display cells (53/0 and 49/3 downward/upward); model-chosen starts are the four-arm study's natural and hide-own arms (4/25 and 12/25). **(B)** Assessment-window welfare (mean and 95% interval) of trajectories whose round-0 state was in the m = 6.0 class, split into tie starts (6.0, 6.0) and capture starts (6.0, 6.5)/(6.5, 6.0). Both start at welfare 311.111. Post hoc and descriptive.

The previous Figure 3 (`fig3-transition-direction.*`, original-run tie-formation counts: LH-natural 45 down / 1 up, HL-natural 57 / 4) is retained in this folder for the supplement but no longer appears in the manuscript.
